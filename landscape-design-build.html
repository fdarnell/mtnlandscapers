You See the Yard Before We Build It

Most landscaping gets sold as a conversation and a number. You describe what you want, someone nods, and you find out what they actually understood once the work is already in the ground.

We draw it first. Every design and build project we take on gets a plan drawn in our design software, so you can look at the layout, move things around and change your mind while changing your mind is still free.

Landscape design and installation across Sevierville, Pigeon Forge, Gatlinburg, Seymour and Kodak. We’re insured and estimates are free.

The Drawing Comes First

A plan does two things. It shows you what you’re buying — where the patio actually sits, how wide the path really is, what the beds look like against the house. And it heads off the expensive kind of mistake, the one where a wall goes in and then has to come back out because nobody worked out where the water goes.

If you want to see it in more depth, we can produce 3D visuals of the finished design for an additional cost. Most people find the plan drawing is enough to make decisions from.

What a Project Includes

A design and build project is the whole yard rather than one element of it, and it can take in any of the following:

  • Hardscape — patios, retaining walls, walkways and steps
  • Planting — beds, shrubs, trees, bed shaping and mulch
  • Lawn — grading and establishing turf from seed or sod
  • Features — landscape lighting, water features and fire features

Because it’s planned as one thing, the pieces get sized against each other. The patio is scaled to the house, the beds are scaled to the patio, and the lighting is planned while there’s still an open trench to run it through — rather than added afterwards at twice the trouble.

All at Once, or a Piece at a Time

Not everyone wants to do the whole yard in one go, and there’s no reason to. We can design the full plan and then build it in stages as it suits you.

The value of planning the whole thing up front is that the early stages don’t get in the way of the later ones. Grade for the patio you’re building next year and you won’t be digging up this year’s lawn to reach it.

One Crew for the Whole Yard

Design and build only works if whoever drew it can also build it. We do the excavation and grading, the drainage, the concrete, the retaining walls, the lighting and the planting.

That matters most in the places where trades usually meet and blame each other. Water, grade and structure are decided by the same people here, working from the same plan — which is exactly where projects normally come apart.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I get to see a design before you start?

Yes. Every design and build project gets a plan drawn in our design software, so you can see the layout and change it before anything is built. 3D visuals of the finished design are available for an additional cost.

Can I do the work in stages?

Yes. We can design the whole yard and build it in phases as it suits you. Planning it all up front means the early stages do not get in the way of the later ones.

What does a design and build project cover?

It can take in hardscape (patios, retaining walls, walkways and steps), planting, lawn from seed or sod, and features such as landscape lighting, water features and fire features.

Do you build what you design, or hand the plan to someone else?

We build it. The same people handle the excavation, drainage, concrete, walls, lighting and planting, which is why water, grade and structure end up agreeing with each other.
