Custom Fire Pit Creations

At Mountain Landscapers, we specialize in crafting custom fire pits that become the heart and community central of your outdoor space. Our expert designers and builders work closely with you to understand exactly what you are looking for such as a:

  1. Simple Wood-Burning Fire Pit: Sometime all you need to add a little something extra to your property is a correctly installed circular pit to stack and burn wood in and enjoy with just a couple people.
  2. Propane Fire Pit: If you need a place to gather around the fire knowing that your source to the flame is simple and refillable, we can help you design the best space for your preferences
  3. Natural Gas Fire Pit: Looking for a more abundant fuel source when it comes to your fire pit gatherings? Big or small we will help you design your own natural gas fueled fire pit so you can burn as much and as long as you need to.
  4. Gel Fire Pit: Looking for an indoor fire pit install or even just a cleaner burning fuel source in your fire pit? We can help with every detail of your modern fire pit design and installation.

Whether your fire pit vision is big, small, or somewhere in between, let us help you make the most of it by talking through different installation options and ideas that would work best for your desired outcome inside your specific gathering space. With years of experience and professional expertise, we are confident we can help!

Our Fire Pit Design and Installation Process

When you choose Mountain Landscapers for your next Fire Pit Design and Installation, you can expect:

When it comes to fire pit design and installation, we follow a meticulous process to ensure the most breathtaking results:

  1. Initial Consultation: We begin with an initial consultation to understand your vision and preferences for the overall project result. We'll discuss your desired layout, material preferences, and overall aesthetic goals.
  2. Site Assessment: In order to prevent unwanted outcomes, our experienced team will conduct a thorough assessment of your landscape to identify any issues that may present themselves during the building process or issues our client may unforeseeably experience after the project is finished.
  3. Customized Design: Based on the consultation and site assessment, our talented designers will create a customized fire pit design that complements the existing landscape and reflects your style. We carefully select all of our materials and consider factors such as seasonal changes, tree shade/clearance, surrounding structural nuances, and more to create a stunning and functional fire pit for years to come.
  4. Quality Materials Selection We believe in using the highest quality materials to ensure long-lasting and durable fire pits. Our team carefully sources a variety a fire pit materials from locally trusted sources, selecting materials known for their texture, color, capabilities, and endurance to East Tennessee's climate.
  5. Installation and Maintenance: Our skilled landscapers will expertly install your fire pit, paying attention to ground clearing, leveling, compacting, pit building, rebar placement, and more. We'll also leave you with a proper understanding of how to care and maintain your new fire pit so that it will last as long as you want it to.
