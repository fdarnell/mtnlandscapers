## 24 Nov 2026, answers typed by the owner

- **Who does the work:** "If we don't have an experienced technician that can do it, we will source from our list of professionals to find the right person for the job."
- **Driveway base:** "A driveway base is typically excavated as needed. After that, it will have soil brought in for any needs pertaining to elevation increases. Next, at least a 4 inch base will be delivered, tamped, and screeded (if needed). Finally, the surface will be installed."
- **Paver driveway offerings** (full driveways, aprons, parking pads, replacing an old driveway): nothing to add or remove.
- **Outdoor kitchen islands:** "We can do any kind of custom island. We can present a range of options."
- **Fire pits:** "We have installed all kinds of fire pits. Some are made of retaining wall block. Some are made of stacked stone. Some are made of concrete. One of the company favorites so far was a completely custom smokeless fire pit. This particular fire pit had custom veneer, custom caps, and was surrounded by an LED flagstone patio."
- **Hardscapes, a project they're proud of:** a back yard with "a stamped and dyed curved wrap-around sidewalk, retaining wall surrounding a low side of the backyard, stamped and dyed concrete wood burning fire pit, putt-putt area, multiple layers of retaining wall, professional grade railing, flagstone step, and hanging lights."
- **Outdoor lighting:** "We install it and we offer Cast Lighting or the client can supply their own lighting gear."
- **Water features:** "We have installed frog ponds, vase-style water features, personal boulder water features, and even water features that wrap around an entire house and have boulder grotto features into a massive pool at the bottom."
- **Warranty:** "Warranties are optional so you don't have to pay for them if you don't want to. All of our work includes a 30-day warranty except for living things."
- **Other towns wanted:** Knoxville.
- **Photos:** in the Mountain Landscapers folder on his computer (~/Desktop/MountainLandscapers; "Edited Photos" and "Product Photos" hold job pictures).
