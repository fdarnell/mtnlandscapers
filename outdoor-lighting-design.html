With the industry's Outdoor Lighting always changing, there are many different kinds of lighting to choose from!

There are many different types of outdoor lighting, each has its own purpose, character, and vibe. Some common types of lighting include:

  • Ambient lighting: This is the general lighting you might see in any random space. It provides enough light to see comfortably, but it doesn't create any specific effects.
  • Task lighting: This is the lighting that is used to illuminate specific tasks, such as reading or working at a desk. For landscaping purposes, an example of a task light would be a spot-light beaming down to place visual emphasis on the backside of a waterfall pond!
  • Accent lighting: This is the lighting that is used to highlight specific features in a space, such as artwork or architectural details. In many cases, accent lights point up, down, left, or right on the face of a wall creating a cone-like feature. This kind of light adds dimension and depth to an otherwise bland surface.
  • Decorative lighting: This is the lighting that is used to create a mood or atmosphere in a space. Many times, designers of home spaces create their decorative lighting to be various colors or even to change colors on demand with a remote! For businesses, decorative lighting is usually simple, elegant, and follows a property's color theme. Don't have a theme? Give us a call to discuss a vision and get a quote!
