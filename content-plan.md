Real work from the estimate history (LMN archive, Jan–Aug 2026), by town.

customer names. Anything not listed here goes in as a TODO for the owner.

- **Gatlinburg:** engineered plans commissioned for a wall; lot clearing; a

- **Seymour:** a pea-gravel walkway with edging; a planting job (dwarf
