Our Landscape Design and Installation Services

Retaining Walls

At Mountain Landscapers, we specialize in crafting customized retaining walls. Our goal is to make retaining walls that not only serve their practical purpose. We want them to enhance the visual beauty of your space. Experienced professionals combine creativity and practical engineering to build our retaining walls. We don't want our retaining walls to only complement the natural slopes of your property. We want our retaining walls to support and serve the surrounding land. We have built functional walls for dirt retention. We have built beautiful, terraced gardens. We have the expertise to get the job done and get it done right.

Fire Pits

Experience warmth and ambiance with our custom fire pit installations. Perfect for chilly evenings, our fire pits add that perfect focal point to your outdoor space. Let us create a cozy gathering spot on your property. From there, you can do your part by making lasting memories with friends and family.

Hardscape Design

If you want to take charge of the potential of your outdoor space, our professional hardscape solutions are what you need. Our experienced teams can create the most captivating, practical hardscapes in any space. Paths, patios and walls only scratch the surface of what we are capable of. Our work will blend exactly how you need it to. Enhance the functionality and visual appeal of your outdoor or even indoor space with the perfect hardscape.

Modern Landscape

Do you embrace contemporary beauty? Our modern landscape designs are what you need. Sleek lines, minimalist features, and artistic elements. Each will play a role in bringing together your outdoor sanctuary.

Seasonal Planting and Flowerbed Design

Celebrate every season with stunning floral displays. Our seasonal planting and flowerbed designs put color and life into your landscape. Not only will it be beautiful when we finish but long after we are gone. Our work will add a touch of natural beauty that evolves with each change of the seasons.

Water Features

Discover serenity with our captivating water features. From tranquil ponds to soothing waterfalls! We incorporate these important elements to create a restful atmosphere. Your water feature will ever flow asking you to come relax and unwind. Let us place you amidst nature's soothing melodies with your own water feature.

Lighting Design and Installation

Bring your outdoor paradise to life with our expert lighting design and installation services. Whether it's for security or ambiance, our well-placed lighting will get the job done. We can enhance the allure of your landscape to bring out exactly how beautiful it is. safety and functionality.

Outdoor Living Spaces

Maximize your living area with our bespoke outdoor spaces. We will get it done right from cozy seating areas to fully equipped kitchens. We curate inviting spaces where you can entertain, relax, and savor the beauty of the great outdoors.

Landscape Fencing

Ensure privacy and security while enhancing your land. Our premium fencing solutions will add aesthetic charm to your perimeter. We offer an array of styles and materials that will complement your outdoor setting with perfection.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1What areas do you cover, and how far will you travel?

The core of our work is Sevier County and the towns around it — Sevierville, Pigeon Forge, Gatlinburg, Kodak, Seymour and Knoxville. Past that it depends on the job. For the right project we will travel further, including out of state, so it is worth asking.

Q2Is there a minimum job size?

No. We will take on a single set of steps as readily as a whole yard.

Q3Do you charge for an estimate?

No. Estimates are free. On a design and build project the simple drawing and project plan are free as well. 3D visuals of the finished design are available for an additional cost.

Q4How soon will I hear back?

We call back the same day.

Q5Do you do the work yourselves, or subcontract it?

We do it ourselves. The same crew handles the excavation, drainage, concrete, walls and planting, which is why the water, the grade and the structure end up agreeing with each other. Where a job needs gas or electrical, that part goes to a certified gas installer and licensed electricians.

Q6How soon can you start?

It varies with the project, mostly according to how many different trades it needs. A patio and some planting schedules faster than a job that has to line up gas, electrical and concrete alongside the landscaping.

Q7Can I do the work in stages?

Yes. We can design the whole yard and build it in phases as it suits you. Planning it all up front means the early stages do not get in the way of the later ones.

Q8How does payment work?

A standard deposit is 50%. On a larger project we set up a payment plan instead, with payments tied to phases of the job being completed.

Q9I do not live in Tennessee. Can you still handle my property?

Yes, and it is routine. A great deal of the property here is owned from out of state, and we are used to running a project for someone who cannot be on site while it happens.
