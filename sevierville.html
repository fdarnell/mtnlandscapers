A Sevierville crew that builds what it designs

Mountain Landscapers is based in Sevierville, and most of our work happens a short drive from town. Over the past year, two out of every three jobs we sold had a Sevierville address. That covers in-town yards, cabin lots on the hillsides, and the farm ground out toward Boyds Creek.

We draw the plan, then the same crew digs it, drains it and builds it. On Sevier County ground that matters. Clay holds water, a lot of lots fall away from the house, and a wall or patio that ignores either one moves within a few seasons.

What we build around Sevierville

  • Retaining walls in Everloc and Redi-Rock block, with drainage pipe, #57 gravel and geofabric behind every one
  • Patios, walkways and steps, plus fire pits and outdoor living areas
  • French drains and grading that move water away from the house
  • Excavation, concrete, planting beds, irrigation and lighting

Recent work around Sevierville

Two block retaining walls this spring

This spring we built two large Everloc block retaining walls in Sevierville. One of them also needed new deck footings and soil testing before the first course went down, so we tested the soil instead of guessing. Walls that hold up a driveway, a deck or a building start with an engineered plan, and we commission those through engineers we work with regularly.

A rental cabin, start to finish

This summer we reworked a whole cabin property: both sides of the driveway, both sides of the cabin, new walls, a putt-putt green, a fire pit and the back yard. Around here a lot of cabins rent, and the yard is the first thing in the listing photos.

Water first

Plenty of Sevierville yards hold water against the house or wash out at the bottom of a slope. We run French drains in perforated 4-inch pipe with pop-up emitters at the outlet, and we shape the grade so the water goes somewhere useful. Most of our retaining wall contracts include drainage, because water behind a wall is what pushes it over.

Fences, lights and finish work

Not every job is a wall. We’ve replaced worn-out fences, put up hog-wire fence with hanging lights, stained decks and drawn lighting plans so a yard still works after dark.

Permits, plans and estimates

In Sevier County a retaining wall needs a permit once it’s going to be more than 4 feet tall. If the wall carries a driveway or a structure, the engineering comes first, and we’ll start that process with you.

Estimates are free. On a design and build project the simple drawing and project plan are free too; 3D visuals of the finished design cost extra. We call back the same day.
