Custom Garden Creations

At Mountain Landscapers, we take a great deal of pride in creating custom garden designs that freshen up your property and give life to your outdoor space. Our team-lead designers and horticulturists (garden designers) listen and work closely with you to understand your garden vision and preferences. Combining creativity with an abundance of knowledge about East Tennessee's always changing climate, we make gardens that reflect your property's style and fit nicely with its natural surroundings.

We Offer a Wide Variety of Garden Design Concepts and Installation ideas in the Sevier County Area

Flower bed design is a true art that we enjoy taking seriously! This art of arranging plants in a way that is both visually appealing and functional is something we are proud of. It can be a daunting task but, with the right people at your side, it can be simple and stress-free.

Some things we like to keep in mind when designing our flower beds include:

  • Color: Choose plants that will complement each other when it comes to color schemes. You can create a monochromatic look with all one color, or you can create a more vibrant look with a variety of colors and pedal designs.
  • Height: Plant taller plants in the back of your flower bed and shorter plants toward the front; this will create a sense of depth and interest.
  • Texture: Mix plants with different textures, such as furry, smooth looking, and spiky. This will add visual interest and make the bed more visually appealing to visitors.
  • Seasonal interest: Choose plants that will bloom at different times throughout the year. This will ensure that your flower bed is always attractive.
  • If you are not sure where to start on taking charge of your garden vision, you can hire a professional flower bed designer here at Mountain Landscapers.
